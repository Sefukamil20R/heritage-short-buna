# DESIGN

## Audience and intent
- **Who:** my family and Ethiopians living abroad who miss home.
- **Where:** on phones, mostly vertical (TikTok), so 9:16 is the main export.
- **Intent:** after watching, they should feel the warmth and ritual of the buna ceremony and feel connected to home.
- **Language:** Amharic narration with English subtitles so non-Amharic speakers can follow.

## User test 1: first cut
- **Who:** 3 family members: my mother, my father and my brother. All three speak Amharic.
- **What they said:**
  - The voice sounds 100% like AI.
  - When the voice pronounces some words differently from how a person would, the meaning changes. Example: "silala" can mean "lightly" or "hardly".
  - The Amharic was otherwise good and clear.
- **What I changed:** I refined the Amharic wording of the lines where the pronunciation changed the meaning and regenerated only those shots.
- **What I did not change:** the AI sound of the voice. This is a limit of the TTS model and I recorded it in FAILURES.md.

## User test 2: refined cut
- **Who:** 3 family members: my mother, my father and my brother
- **What they said:** they said this version was better.
- **What changed because of it:** nothing ,  Remaining problem: the voice still sounds like AI.
